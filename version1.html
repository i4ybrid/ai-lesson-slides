What is AI? 🤔

AI = a computer doing things that normally need a human brain.

Seeing 👀 Reading 📖 Talking 🗨️ Guessing 🎯

ChatGPT is an LLM — it read billions of pages and learned ONE skill: guess the next word.

It's not real thinking... but it's good enough to treat it like a robot friend. 🤝

The dangers of AI 🚨

  • Remember: AI guesses. Guesses can be wrong.
  • It invents fake facts — grown-ups call it a "hallucination" 👻
  • The tricky part: it says its guesses like it KNOWS they're true 😤
  • Like fire: useful AND dangerous 🔥

Why go to school? Don't be a replaceable human 🦾

  • Be honest: if AI can do everything you can do... what will your job be?
  • A human AI can fully replace is a human who isn't in control of their own destiny 🎮
  • School trains your brain to think, focus and not give up — that's what makes you irreplaceable
  • 👫 Plus: friends, teachers, recess — things no robot gets

Don't be a useless human. Stay the one holding the controller.
